# Code of conduct

Participants must treat each other with respect. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and unwanted sexual attention are prohibited. Keep discussion constructive and focused on the work. Respect privacy and stop conduct when asked.

Ryan Grippeling (@Ryangr0) is the initial enforcement contact. Ask for a private channel without posting sensitive details publicly. If the report concerns that maintainer, an independent reporting and appeal contact must be established; it is not yet available. Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation, document the reason privately, and hear an appeal through an uninvolved reviewer when one is available.

A formal community policy with an independent reporting route remains part of CNCF preparation. This initial policy does not claim compliance with CNCF onboarding.
